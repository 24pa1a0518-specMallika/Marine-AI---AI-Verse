# Marine AI Data Sources

## Weather

Provides:

- Wind speed
- Wind direction
- Rain probability
- Lightning

Possible sources:

- IMD
- Weather APIs
- Mock data for prototype

---

## Ocean

Provides:

- Wave height
- Wave period
- Sea surface temperature
- Chlorophyll
- Ocean currents

Possible sources:

- INCOIS
- MOSDAC
- ERDDAP

---

## PFZ

Provides:

- Potential Fishing Zone locations
- Latitude
- Longitude
- Confidence/probability
- Supporting ocean parameters

Source:

- INCOIS PFZ

---

## Hazards

Provides:

- Cyclone information
- High wave warnings
- Storm surge
- Marine warnings

Possible sources:

- IMD
- INCOIS

---

## Geospatial

Provides:

- EEZ boundaries
- Restricted zones
- Marine protected areas
- Fishing zones

Possible sources:

- INCOIS
- Government GIS datasets
- GeoJSON prototype data
